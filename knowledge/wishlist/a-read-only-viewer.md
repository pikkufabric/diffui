# A read-only viewer

A person who can read a project's reports but cannot declare routes, push shots
or re-pin a baseline — a manager watching a migration, someone in another team.

Not built, because [only `engineer` was asked for](../decisions/one-app-orgs-for-tenancy.md)
and an invented role becomes invented screens and invented rules.

Worth noting that Better Auth's organisation plugin already carries member-level
roles, so this may turn out to be a permission rather than a new system role.
